GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Is TaskBit available for Android? ＋

TaskBit is an Android app. The official APK link will appear here once the final package is ready.

How do task rewards work? ＋

Each task shows its instructions in the app. Rewards are recorded only after the backend confirms the task completion. Available tasks and reward terms may change.

What information does the app use? ＋

TaskBit uses account details such as your name and mobile number, task activity, and reward balances. Payout details are used only when you add them for a withdrawal request.

Where can I get help? ＋

For help, use the support or contact option provided inside the TaskBit app.
